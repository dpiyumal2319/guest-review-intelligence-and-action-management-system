# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Hotel general managers, department managers, and demo administrators use this academic prototype during operational review and assessment workflows. They need to inspect guest feedback, identify recurring operational issues, route ownership to the correct department, and track corrective action without losing the review evidence behind each ticket.

## Product Purpose
Guest Review Intelligence turns fragmented hotel guest feedback into structured operational insight and department-owned action tickets. It exists to prove an end-to-end academic prototype: ingest source-shaped review data, normalize and analyze it, surface recurring issues and Reputation Risk, and support manual corrective-action workflows that managers can review, update, resolve, and verify.

## Brand Personality
Calm, credible, operational. The interface should feel like a trustworthy internal hotel operations tool: clear enough for a live university demo, restrained enough that data and workflow states remain the focus, and polished enough to communicate rigor rather than startup theatrics.

## Anti-references
Do not make this feel like a marketing SaaS landing page, a consumer travel app, or an over-decorated analytics toy. Avoid speculative AI aesthetics, noisy gradients, decorative dashboards, or workflow patterns that hide evidence and traceability behind style.

## Design Principles
- Keep the review evidence close to the decision so managers can justify every ticket they create or update.
- Make operational ownership explicit through department, priority, assignee, and lifecycle state on every ticket surface.
- Prefer familiar dashboard and workflow patterns over novel interactions that would slow task completion.
- Preserve auditability by showing meaningful lifecycle history instead of silent state mutation.
- Use restrained visual emphasis so Reputation Risk, ticket status, and actionability stand out without turning the UI into a warning wall.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-reachable workflow controls across review, issue, and ticket surfaces. Respect reduced-motion preferences, avoid color-only state communication, and keep labels, event history, and workflow copy explicit enough for mixed technical literacy across demo stakeholders.
